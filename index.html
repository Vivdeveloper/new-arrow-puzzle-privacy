Privacy Policy — New Arrow Puzzle Game

Last updated: October 2026

This policy describes New Arrow Puzzle Game as built today. If the app changes (for example, if new features are added), this document and the in-app policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

What we store

Your level progress, stars, best scores, statistics and settings are saved only on this device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

What we don’t collect

The game has no accounts and plays offline. It doesn’t collect your name, email, location, contacts, advertising ID or any device identifiers.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

When online, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, such as which level was played, stars, moves and time, plus basic app and device information (app version, device model, OS version, country). They are tied only to a random app-instance ID, never to you, and help us balance levels and fix bugs. Advertising ID collection and ad personalisation are turned off. The game also checks Google Play for updates. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Anonymous statistics.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the level list and the pause menu, an ad card marked “Ad” at the end of the Stats screen, an occasional ad between levels, and a short video you can choose to watch for an extra hint or an extra life. Ads never appear while you play. Every ad request is marked as directed to children, so ads are non-personalised, rated suitable for all ages (G), and don’t use the advertising ID. To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may process your IP address and basic device information. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Permissions

Vibration is used for haptic feedback during play. Notifications are optional: if you allow them, the game shows up to three daily puzzle reminders, scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time. The game doesn’t use the microphone, camera, or location.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset Progress to clear your progress, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Your privacy choices

In Settings → Privacy you can turn anonymous statistics off or on at any time. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google’s consent message the first time it starts, and Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices lets you review or change that choice whenever you like. Elsewhere that option isn’t shown, because ads are always non-personalised. Notifications, sound and vibration can also be turned off in Settings.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the CCPA in California or the DPDP Act in India), you can ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to or limit how it’s used, and withdraw your consent. We hold no personal data about you: there are no accounts, your progress stays on your device, and statistics are anonymous and can’t be linked to you. You can delete everything yourself with Reset Progress or by uninstalling. We never sell or share personal information, and it’s never used for targeted advertising. To make a request or ask a question, write to choudharyvivek195@gmail.com and we’ll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone. Gameplay statistics are anonymous, and ads are child-directed, non-personalised and rated for all ages.

Contact

Changes
